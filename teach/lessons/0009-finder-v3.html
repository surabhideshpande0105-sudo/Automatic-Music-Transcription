Lesson 0009 · 8 min · Why high notes and mixes improved

Separate, track, harmonize

Three upgrades replaced max-magnitude picking. HPSS splits sustained tones from drum hits so pitched lines survive mixes. pyin tracks pitch probabilistically with smoothing instead of jumping to the loudest bin. A residual voice masks the lead's harmonics and reads the strongest remaining peak as one chord tone.

The octave guard matters most: pyin period-doubles on chords (reports C2 for C4), which used to poison masking. Now the lead is lifted toward the spectral peak before masking — C2 ghosts gone, chord C4+E4 found every slice.

Limits to remember: 2 voices max, rare deep-bass octave slip. Full polyphony still belongs to Basic Pitch on Python 3.11.
