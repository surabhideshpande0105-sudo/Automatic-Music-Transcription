MIDI bytes become a score

write_midi (amt_score.py:19) packs note on/off events with variable-length ticks at 480 ticks/beat, 120 BPM — pure stdlib, no dependency. midi_to_musicxml (amt_score.py:44) then asks music21 to quantize time into measures, clef, and noteheads.

Side-quest, not transcription: train_model.py:86 train() learns Markov MIDI→MIDI transitions from MAESTRO CSVs and generates new MIDIs. Useful for data, not for audio.
