Lesson 0006 · 7 min · The fix behind your Chariots score

One reversed loop broke every timestamp

MIDI stores time gaps as VLQ bytes: 7 data bits per byte, most-significant group first, every byte but the last flagged with a top bit. Our _varlen (amt_score.py:7) emitted the groups backwards. Values under 128 looked fine; anything bigger decoded as garbage — 19200 ticks read back as 360449, so a 20s note replayed as 375s.

Same bug lived in train_model.py:188 — every generated MIDI was affected, not just transcriptions.

Rule learned: roundtrip every writer through an independent reader. The test that caught it encodes 0…360449 and decodes with readable.py — now zero failures.
