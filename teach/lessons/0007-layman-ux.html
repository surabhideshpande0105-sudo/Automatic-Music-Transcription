Three steps, one button

The rebuild of app.py follows one rule: the user never sees a jargon word or a file picker they didn't ask for. Pick music → press Transcribe my music → listen, read, download. The score renders inside the page via OSMD, and a sonified MIDI lets ears verify what eyes see.

Compare-by-ear is the trust device: original audio beside the computer's replay of what it heard.

Engineering follows UX: transcribe_file_auto() returns (notes, engine) so the UI can name the engine honestly, and failures surface as friendly warnings, never tracebacks.
