Audio in, notation out

AMT turns sound into a note list (pitch + onset + duration), then into notation. Your project does this in two formats: .mid for performance, .musicxml for the printed score.

Polyphonic = many pitches at once (piano chords). Monophonic = one at a time (your librosa placeholder). Your target is polyphonic piano.

Latency split: MIDI onsets can stream at ~200–500ms. Full notation waits 1–2s for beat/key context. That is why test_latency.py checks I/O only — the model costs extra.
